Week of: September 20, 2026
My goal this week

My goal this week was to plan and start building my TradeJournal website/app. I wanted to create an app where I could record my trades and review my wins, losses, and trading patterns over time.

What I did

I started by planning the main idea of TradeJournal and identifying its purpose and target user. I decided that the app would let me record the ticker or asset, entry price, exit price, position size, date, outcome, and notes for each trade.

I also planned the main screens of the app, including the Dashboard, Trade Journal, Trade Details, and Statistics pages. After planning the features, I started creating the actual website/app and added features for logging trades, viewing saved trades, checking win/loss statistics, and viewing trade details.

What blocked me

One problem I had was deciding how to organize the different screens and features of the app. I also had to think about how the trade information should be stored and how the statistics should update when I add or remove a trade.

What I learned

I learned that planning the screens and data first makes building the app easier. I also learned how the information entered by the user can be stored and used to show statistics such as total trades, wins, losses, and win rate. This helped me understand how the different parts of a React app work together.
